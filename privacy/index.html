WordSpeed Teleprompter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

WordSpeed Teleprompter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No network access. The app never connects to the internet. It has no servers, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising.
  • No account. There is no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync.
  • Your scripts stay on your device. Text you type or import is stored only in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WordSpeed on your own computer. It is never uploaded.
  • Files are read only when you open them. The app reads the document you explicitly choose (txt, md, html, rtf, pdf, docx) and nothing else. It does not scan your disks, your clipboard history, or other applications.
  • No third parties. No SDKs, no trackers, no data brokers.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete. Uninstalling the app and deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\WordSpeed removes everything it ever wrote.

WordSpeed 提词器不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人数据。

  • 不联网。本应用从不连接互联网，没有服务器、没有统计、没有遥测、没有崩溃上报、没有广告。
  • 不需要账号。没有注册、没有登录、没有云同步。

  • 只读取你主动打开的文件。应用只读取你选择的文档（txt、md、html、rtf、pdf、docx），不扫描磁盘、不读取剪贴板历史、不访问其它程序。
  • 没有第三方。不集成任何第三方 SDK、追踪器或数据代理。

因为不收集任何数据，所以没有可供查询、更正、导出或删除的内容。卸载应用并删除 %LOCALAPPDATA%\WordSpeed 目录，即可清除它写入过的全部内容。
